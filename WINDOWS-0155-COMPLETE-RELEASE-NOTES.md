## Scope and limitations

- Offline speech recognition is real, tested and available after one-file installation; short publicly licensed Burmese test audio was used. This does NOT certify accurate 90-minute author's novel speech alignment.
- The available local-artwork matching suggests ONLY already imported, label-matching backgrounds for important scenes; no proprietary online image browsing or automatic AI picture generation is included.
- The scene timing and speech models remain human-review-only. Ordinary rain, dialogue and minor character entrance should not generate slideshow-like picture changes.
- Project text, voice, photos and backups remain local; do not put unpublished novels or audio in public GitHub.
- Digital signing, full novel semantic understanding, Android app, Google Drive private sync, and personal laptop acceptance are not complete.
